# SolidWorks Projects Portfolio

A collection of 3D mechanical models and designs created using SolidWorks as part of my 
engineering practice and self-directed learning.

**Author:** Muhammad Hashir Rauf  
**Degree:** BS Mechatronics Engineering — Air University, Islamabad  
**Tool:** SolidWorks 2025  

---

## Folders

### 📁 [Bearing Supports](./bearing-supports)
Bearing housing and support components including pillow blocks, 
pedestal mounts, and flanged housings.

### 📁 [Brackets and Mounts](./brackets-and-mounts)
Various mechanical brackets, wall mounts, and base mounting components.

### 📁 [Linkages and Joints](./linkages-and-joints)
Mechanical linkages, crank arms, pivot joints, and rocker arms.

### 📁 [Plates and Connectors](./plates-and-connectors)
Base plates, hinge connectors, and multi-arm spider plates.

### 📁 [Mechanical Components](./mechanical-components)
Standard mechanical parts including spur gears, pipe elbows, 
and complex multi-feature components.

### 📁 [Consumer Products](./consumer-products)
Everyday product models including a smartphone, wristwatch, pencil, and cake.

### 📁 [Creative and Advanced](./creative-and-advanced)
Advanced and creative models including a carbon nanotube structure, 
panda logo emblem, and surface modeling practice parts.
